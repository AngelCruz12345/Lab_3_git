Elena stared at the wall.

For a moment, she couldn't move.

The green code continued to ripple across the highway, replacing asphalt with endless columns of symbols. Beyond it, the matte-black barrier rose into a darkness that seemed to have no sky above it.

Then the voice spoke again.

"Do not exit the vehicle."

Elena's hands tightened around the steering wheel.

"Who are you?"

There was a pause.

"Your route supervisor."

"That's not an answer."

Another pause.

Then, quietly:

"It is the only answer you have been permitted to hear."

The dashboard flickered. A map appeared—not of Route 9, not of the coast, but of an enormous circular structure. Thousands of roads formed concentric rings around a glowing center.

Her position blinked on the outermost ring.

Elena zoomed out.

The circle was surrounded by nothing.

No cities. No oceans. No countries.

Just black.

She swallowed.

"How long have I been driving?"

The synthetic voice did not respond immediately.

Instead, the odometer began changing.

43,000 miles.

89,000.

212,000.

1,000,000.

The numbers accelerated until the display became a blur.

"Stop."

The odometer froze.

7,846,291 miles.

Elena stared at it.

"That's impossible."

"It was."

The driver's door unlocked.

A thin line of white light appeared around the edge.

Elena looked toward the barrier. For the first time, she noticed a small opening in its surface.

Something was standing on the other side.

A human figure.

The figure raised one hand.

Elena's headlights illuminated its face.

It was her.

Older.

Much older.

Gray hair framed a face marked by decades of exhaustion. But the eyes were unmistakable.

Her own eyes.

The older Elena pressed her palm against the black wall.

The synthetic voice returned.

"Sector 4 containment has failed."

The older woman mouthed four words through the glass.

You have to remember.

Then the lights went out.

For three seconds, Elena was surrounded by absolute darkness.

When the headlights returned, the figure was gone.

So was the wall.

In its place stood a ruined coastal city beneath a blood-red sky.

And scattered across the abandoned highway were thousands of identical electric sedans.

Every one of them had its headlights on.

Every one contained a driver.

Every driver was Elena.
